Job listings and applications

Oilfield Jobs helps you browse and save public job listings and keep a manual record of applications you have made. Listings come from external sources and may change or close. Job information may be extracted, structured or summarised using AI. The text shown in the app can differ from the source and may contain extraction errors or omit details. Use View original job to check the current role, location, qualifications, deadlines and application instructions. The recruitment site or employer controls its application process and hiring decisions.

After applying through the original source, you can sign in and confirm Mark as Applied to add a record to Applications. We store the job information and time you marked it with your account so you can view the record across devices. Opening a recruitment site does not submit an application or automatically mark it as applied. The app does not verify your submission or retrieve a hiring decision from the employer.

Job alerts

Job alerts are optional and off by default. When available, you can turn on a daily digest from Profile → Job alerts, choose a region, one or more categories, or both, and grant system notification permission. The digest is scheduled around 8:00 a.m. in your device’s local time and is sent only when there are matching new listings. Operating-system settings, connectivity and delivery services can affect timing or delivery. Check the original job posting for application deadlines. You can turn alerts off in the app or manage permissions in your device settings.
