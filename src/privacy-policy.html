This notice describes the Oilfield Jobs website. For the app’s privacy information, open the Privacy Policy in the app’s Profile section.

Website analytics

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics measures page visits and which download channel visitors open. Google Analytics can use cookies and browser or device information to produce usage reports. The site saves your analytics choice in this browser. You can change it under Analytics settings at the bottom of the homepage.

Google explains how it processes analytics data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

External links and support

When you visit a store or an external download page, its operator handles your visit under its own policies. If you email us, we use your message and reply address to help with your request.

For privacy questions, email support@eaxmarketplace.com. For account deletion, follow the instructions on the Delete account page to send your request to support@eaxmarketplace.com.
