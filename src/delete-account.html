Delete your Oilfield Jobs account

Request deletion by email

Email support@eaxmarketplace.com from the email address associated with your Oilfield Jobs account. Include that you want to delete your Oilfield Jobs account and linked data. We may need to confirm that the account is yours before processing the request.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to Oilfield Jobs.
  2. Open Profile → Account → Delete account.
  3. Follow the confirmation prompt.

What deletion covers

Account deletion removes account-linked information, including read history, saved jobs, job preferences and manually recorded applications, and ends your sign-in sessions. Alerts linked to the account’s sessions are disabled.

Independent device settings and guest records are separate. Alerts enabled for the device without an account can remain active until you turn them off. Account deletion does not automatically remove Analytics or Crashlytics information already received by Google.

Keep copies of any records you need before deleting the account. Read the Oilfield Jobs Privacy Policy for the full deletion and retention details, including information that may be retained for legal obligations or disputes. Contact support@eaxmarketplace.com for help with a data-deletion request.
