Common questions

Do I apply inside the app?

Use View original job to open the source and apply through the employer or recruiter.

Do I need an account to browse?

You can browse the Jobs feed and open job details as a guest.

Can I focus on a particular region?

Choose from West Africa, Asia Pacific, the North Sea, the Middle East, North America or Latin America in Job preferences, then open Preferred. Latest shows the full list.

How can I tell which jobs I’ve read?

Each date group shows how many jobs are unread and how many were posted. Opening a job clears its unread dot. You can still revisit the listing.

When can I use the AI tools?

Job match, resume tools and application materials are planned for a future release.
