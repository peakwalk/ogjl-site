Oilfield Jobs Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026
Updated: 26 November 2026 (app analytics and crash reporting)

This Privacy Policy explains how Peakwalk Info Tech Ltd. (“Peakwalk,” “we,” or “us”) handles personal information when you use Oilfield Jobs on iOS or Android. You can browse public job listings without creating an account. Some features described below become available when enabled for your app version or region.

Job browsing, preferences and saved jobs

Without an account, read markings, saved jobs and job preferences are kept on your device. Search filters apply to that search and do not change your job preferences. Your theme choice stays on the device.

When you sign in, eligible read markings and saved jobs from the device are automatically brought into that account. Read markings or saved jobs already assigned to one account are not automatically imported into a different account. Once a saved job is successfully imported, it is removed from the device's guest Saved list and can be found in the receiving account. Any saved jobs still in the guest list remain separate from account lists.

We store account read history, saved jobs and job preferences so that the same account can use them across devices. If the account has never set job preferences, it can start with the preferences on the device. If the account already has preferences, those take priority. Clearing account preferences keeps that choice; signing in again does not replace it with older device preferences. Markings made while offline can be sent to the same account when the connection returns. Saving account preferences or adding and removing account saved jobs requires a connection; this does not promise an offline queue for those changes.

Signing out keeps read markings made on that device. Markings received only from another device remain account data and do not become guest read markings. Signing out does not delete the account's stored content. Switching accounts shows the receiving account's own read history, saved jobs and preferences instead of carrying over the previous account's content.

AI features

Before an AI action sends resume text, the app asks you to confirm. Resume optimisation uses text from the version you select. Tailoring and cover-letter drafting use that text and relevant public job information. Match scoring compares the current resume version with the public job information. Our service sends those inputs to the AI provider configured for the feature. The extracted text may include personal information from your resume. Use a resume version containing only information you are comfortable sending for that request.

AI match scores and explanations are for your own use. Oilfield Jobs does not send those scores, your resume or your saved cover letter to employers as part of browsing, generating materials or opening a listing. The app lets you copy or export materials and choose where to share them. A website, recipient or sharing service you choose handles the copy under its own practices.

Analytics and crash reports

In app versions that include Google Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, the two sharing options start on and are stored on your device, separately from your account. You can change either option in Profile → Privacy & Data, including when you are not signed in. We use app usage statistics and technical crash diagnostics to improve Oilfield Jobs. We do not enable advertising identifiers, advertising personalisation or advertising attribution for these services.

Analytics changes apply immediately. Changes to crash reporting take effect after restarting the app. Turning a sharing option off does not automatically delete information already received by the service. Contact us if you need help with a data request. Google processes the information received by these services under its applicable terms and privacy practices. These diagnostic settings do not control your account, saved jobs or job alert notifications.

Analytics may derive a general location, such as a country or region, from a masked IP address. This is separate from the job region you choose and does not require GPS access. You can stop future Analytics collection using the Analytics switch described above.

Who receives information

  • Service providers. Hosting and storage providers support the service. Google provides Google Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for the app versions described above. An email provider may deliver account messages. The configured AI provider receives the inputs needed for a confirmed AI request.
  • Sign-in, billing and delivery services. Google and Apple process information when you use their sign-in or billing services. To link a purchase to your account, we provide the store with your Oilfield Jobs account ID or a store-specific identifier derived from it. If you enable job alerts, our backend sends the push token and notification payload directly to Apple Push Notification service (APNs) for iOS or Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android to deliver the digest.
  • Recruitment websites. When you follow a link to a job source such as Rigzone, the website you open handles your visit and any information you submit there under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal and safety purposes. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect people or the service, investigate fraud or enforce our rights.

Depending on where you use the app and where our providers operate, information may be processed outside your country. Where applicable, we use the safeguards required for such transfers. You can contact us for information about the providers and safeguards relevant to your use of the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information and saved account content while the account is active, subject to the deletion controls described below. We keep temporary verification, security and delivery information for the time needed to complete the request, prevent abuse or troubleshoot delivery. Unsaved resume optimisation and tailoring drafts held by our service expire after 24 hours. A generated cover letter is shown in the editor. It becomes part of your application pack only when you save it.

You can delete resume versions from your library and remove prepared materials from the app. To delete your account, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. You can also contact us using the address below for help with deletion. Once account deletion is complete, we remove account-linked content and end your sign-in sessions. We also revoke Sign in with Apple authorisation for Oilfield Jobs if it is linked to the account. Deleted files may take additional time to clear from storage. Information may be retained where necessary for legal obligations or disputes. Apple, Google, external job sites and recipients handle their own records. Keep copies of anything you want before deleting it.

Device-based settings and files you previously exported are separate from your account. Deleting your account leaves device-based alerts enabled if you had turned them on. To stop daily alerts, turn them off in Profile → Job alerts. Once that change reaches our service, we stop scheduling new alerts for that device and remove its active push token. We may retain the device’s alert settings and delivery records to manage the service. You can also turn off notification permissions in your device settings. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or cancel a store subscription.

Your choices and rights

You can browse without an account, edit or clear your preferences, choose whether to confirm an AI request, and decide whether to enable alerts. Where applicable law gives you the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to restrict or object to certain uses, contact us to make a request. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Where applicable, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children, security and updates

Oilfield Jobs is intended for job seekers and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

We use private file storage and account access controls to help protect account content. We will post revisions to this policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice where required.
