Oilfield Jobs Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Updated: 27 November 2026 (current features and account records)

This Privacy Policy explains how Peakwalk Info Tech Ltd. (“Peakwalk,” “we,” or “us”) handles personal information when you use Oilfield Jobs on iOS or Android. You can browse public job listings without creating an account. It describes the app’s currently available features. Resume uploads, AI resume tools, match scores, prepared application materials and paid subscriptions are not available in this release.

Job browsing and account records

Without an account, read markings, saved jobs and job preferences are kept on your device. Search filters apply to that search and do not change your job preferences. Your theme choice stays on the device.

When you sign in, eligible read markings and saved jobs from the device are automatically brought into that account. Read markings or saved jobs already assigned to one account are not automatically imported into a different account. Once a saved job is successfully imported, it is removed from the device's guest Saved list and can be found in the receiving account. Any saved jobs still in the guest list remain separate from account lists.

We store account read history, saved jobs, job preferences and manually recorded applications so that the same account can use them across devices. If the account has never set job preferences, it can start with the preferences on the device. If the account already has preferences, those take priority. Clearing account preferences keeps that choice; signing in again does not replace it with older device preferences. Read markings made while offline can be sent to the same account when the connection returns. Recording an application, saving account preferences, or adding and removing account saved jobs requires a connection; this does not promise an offline queue for those changes.

Signing out keeps read markings made on that device. Markings received only from another device remain account data and do not become guest read markings. Signing out does not delete the account's stored content. Switching accounts shows the receiving account's own read history, saved jobs, preferences and manually recorded applications instead of carrying over the previous account's content.

Public job information and AI processing

Our service may use AI to extract and organise information from public job listings. AI providers used for this work receive the public job content needed for that processing. The information shown in the app can differ from the source or contain extraction errors. Use View original job to check the source before applying.

This processing is separate from AI Tools, which is disabled and labelled Coming Soon in this release. The app does not offer resume uploads or personal AI resume and match-score requests in this release. We will update the relevant privacy information before enabling those features. When you open a job source, that website handles information you provide there under its own privacy policy.

Analytics and crash reports

In app versions that include Google Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, the two sharing options start on and are stored on your device, separately from your account. You can change either option in Profile → Privacy & Data, including when you are not signed in. We use app usage statistics and technical crash diagnostics to improve Oilfield Jobs. We do not enable advertising identifiers, advertising personalisation or advertising attribution for these services.

Analytics changes apply immediately. Changes to crash reporting take effect after restarting the app. Turning a sharing option off does not automatically delete information already received by the service. Contact us if you need help with a data request. Google processes the information received by these services under its applicable terms and privacy practices. These diagnostic settings do not control your account, saved jobs or job alert notifications.

Analytics may derive a general location, such as a country or region, from IP address information. This is separate from the job region you choose and does not require GPS access. You can stop future Analytics collection using the Analytics switch described above.

Who receives information

  • Service providers. Hosting and storage providers support the service. Google provides Google Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for app versions that include the sharing options described above. An email provider may deliver account messages. AI providers may process public job information as described above.
  • Sign-in and notification delivery services. Google and Apple process information when you use their sign-in services. If you enable job alerts, our backend sends the push token and notification payload directly to Apple Push Notification service (APNs) for iOS or Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android to deliver the digest.
  • Recruitment websites. When you follow a link to a job source such as Rigzone, the website you open handles your visit and any information you submit there under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal and safety purposes. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect people or the service, investigate fraud or enforce our rights.

Depending on where you use the app and where our providers operate, information may be processed outside your country. Where applicable, we use the safeguards required for such transfers. You can contact us for information about the providers and safeguards relevant to your use of the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information and saved account content while the account is active, subject to the deletion controls described below. We keep temporary verification, security and delivery information for the time needed to complete the request, prevent abuse or troubleshoot delivery.

To delete your account, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. You can also contact us using the address below for help with deletion. Once account deletion is complete, we remove account-linked information, including read history, saved jobs, job preferences and manually recorded applications, and end your sign-in sessions. Alerts linked to the account’s sessions are disabled. We also revoke Sign in with Apple authorisation for Oilfield Jobs if it is linked to the account. Information may be retained where necessary for legal obligations or disputes. Apple, Google and external job sites handle their own records. Keep copies of anything you want before deleting it.

Device-based settings and guest records are separate from your account. Deleting an account does not automatically clear those device choices or remove Analytics and Crashlytics information already received by Google. Alerts independently enabled for the device without an account can remain active. To stop those daily alerts, turn them off in Profile → Job alerts. Once that change reaches our service, we stop scheduling new alerts for that device and remove its active push token. We may retain the device’s alert settings and delivery records to manage the service. You can also turn off notification permissions in your device settings. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Your choices and rights

You can browse without an account, edit or clear your preferences, manage saved jobs, change Analytics and crash reporting settings, and decide whether to enable alerts. Where applicable law gives you the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to restrict or object to certain uses, contact us to make a request. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it for future processing. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Where applicable, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children, security and updates

Oilfield Jobs is intended for job seekers and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

We use private storage and account access controls to help protect account content. We will post revisions to this policy with an updated date and provide additional notice where required.
